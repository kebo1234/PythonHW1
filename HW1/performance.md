# Interpretation of Results

The Momentum strategy outperforms the Mean Reversion strategy across all metrics. Momentum's positive total returns are superior to Mean Reversion's negative total returns, and its slightly positive Sharpe ratio indicates modest risk-adjusted gains, whereas Mean Reversion’s negative Sharpe ratio reflects losses on a risk-adjusted basis. Momentum also demonstrated better capital preservation, with lower maximum drawdown and smaller expected shortfall. It is also important to note that while VaR at 95% is slightly more negative for Momentum, this alone does not capture overall tail risk or cumulative performance. Overall, the Momentum strategy proved to be stronger with higher total returns, positive risk-adjusted returns, and stronger downside protection, suggesting potential for alpha generation.
